Privacy notice

Last updated 10 November 2026.

In short

  • You can use LetsGetFit without an account. Your details, food diary, workouts and weight log are then saved only in your browser, on your device.
  • Without an account, the only things sent to our server are the text you type into food search and, if you choose to scan a meal, the photo you confirm. Neither is stored with anything about you.
  • There is no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party scripts.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following in your browser's local storage so it can work without a server:

  • Your details: age, height and weight, the coefficient used in the energy equation, your usual activity level, and your answer about pregnancy or breastfeeding. These are used to calculate estimates and to check whether the calculator suits you.
  • Your food diary and saved foods, workouts and training sessions, and your weight log.
  • Your unit preference.

This data does not leave your device. It is removed if you use Settings → Clear all data or clear your browser's data. You can download a copy at any time from Settings.

Food search

When you search for a food, your browser sends the search text to letsgetfit.site. Our server forwards it to USDA FoodData Central, a public database run by the U.S. Department of Agriculture, and returns the results.

Search text is not logged. Results are cached by the words searched for, with nothing linking them to you. To prevent abuse, your IP address is used to count searches. It is stored only as a one-way keyed hash, and those records are deleted within a day.

Meal photo scan and food descriptions (when available)

With Food → Describe you can type what you ate instead. When you press “Find the foods”, the text goes to our server and then to the same AI service (named on that screen), which lists the foods and estimates amounts in grams. Nutrients come from USDA FoodData Central, never from the AI. We do not store or log the description.

If photo scanning is switched on and you choose to use it, the photo you select is sent to our server only after you confirm. Your browser and our server first remove location, camera and date details. Our server then sends the photo to one AI service, which suggests the foods and amounts: OpenAI's API, or Anthropic's Claude API. The scan screen tells you which one before you confirm. If the site is comparing the two, a photo goes to only one of them. You check and correct the suggestions, and the nutrients come from USDA FoodData Central for the foods and amounts you confirm.

We do not store the photo. The suggested foods are kept for 10 minutes so that pressing the button twice does not send the photo twice. OpenAI and Anthropic each state that they do not use API data to train their models by default, and that they may keep it for a limited time (OpenAI: up to 30 days) to monitor for abuse. For each scan we keep only counts for the site owner (how many scans, how long they took, tokens used, and how many suggestions were kept or changed), never the photo or the foods. Do not send photos of people or anything private. You can always add food yourself instead.

App and notifications

If you install LetsGetFit as an app, your device keeps a copy of the app's pages, scripts and icons so it opens offline. Your own data is stored the same way as in the browser.

Notifications are off until you turn them on in Settings. When you do, your browser gives us an address at its push service (run by Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your browser) and keys to encrypt messages. We store that address, the reminder times you choose, and your time zone, so we know when to send reminders. Messages are encrypted; the push service only passes them on. We do not store anything else about you for this. If you are logged in, the device is linked to your account and deleted with it. Turn notifications off in Settings at any time, and we delete the address.

Web server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, page requested and browser type) for security and troubleshooting. These are kept according to the provider's own retention policy.

Optional accounts (when available)

You never need an account. If accounts are switched on and you choose to join, we store:

  • Your email address, to send you 6-digit login codes. Codes are stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form, expire after 10 minutes and work once.
  • A password, if you add one, stored only as a one-way hash.
  • The data you choose to sync (your details, weight log, food diary, saved foods, workouts and preferences), only from devices where you turn on sync, and only after you give explicit consent when you join.
  • When you joined and last logged in, and a short record of account actions (such as a password change) without their contents.
  • A session cookie that keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. It is strictly necessary and not used for tracking.

The people who run LetsGetFit can see members' email addresses and account dates to run the service and stop abuse. They do not see your synced health and fitness data. Login emails are sent through our mail server.

You can download everything in your account, log out on every device, or delete your account at any time from the Account page. Deleting it removes your synced data from our database straight away; backups roll over within 30 days. Data on your devices is not changed.

Health information

LetsGetFit gives general estimates, not medical advice. Talk to your GP or a registered dietitian about your own needs.
