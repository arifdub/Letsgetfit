    Units

    Metric is the default. Imperial values are converted to metric once and stored in metric.

    Resting energy use falls slightly with age. The calculator is for adults aged 20 to 78.

    Height

    Taller bodies use more energy at rest. Height is also needed for BMI.

    cm

    Current weight

    Body weight is the largest single input to resting energy. It is also needed for BMI and the protein example.

    kg

    Equation coefficient

    The Mifflin–St Jeor equation was developed with one constant for women (−161 kcal) and one for men (+5 kcal). Choose the one that best matches your body for this estimate. A dietitian or GP can give a more personal estimate if neither fits.

    Usual activity in a typical week

    Include your job, getting around and any regular exercise. Your usual exercise is counted here. The Dashboard can account for extra exercise separately.

    Are you pregnant or breastfeeding?

    Energy and nutrient needs change during pregnancy and breastfeeding, and this calculator does not cover them.

          Your data on this device

          Your details, food diary, workouts, training sessions and weight log are saved in this browser's local storage on this device. They are copied to a server only if you join with an optional account and turn on sync on this device. There is no analytics or tracking. The only thing sent to our server is the text you type into food search, which is passed on to USDA FoodData Central and not stored with anything about you.

          • Clearing your browser data, or using a private window, removes it.
          • It is not shared between browsers or devices.
          • Anyone who uses this browser profile can see it.

          Download as spreadsheets (CSV)

          CSV files open in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. Weights are in kg and amounts in grams.
