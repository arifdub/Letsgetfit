## Accounts are optional

You can use every feature without an account. If you create one, we store the information below so you can use LetsGetFit on more than one device.

## What we store, and why

- **Your email address and a scrambled form of your password.** We use these to let you log in and to send you confirmation and password-reset emails. We never see or store your actual password. Legal basis: providing the service you asked for.
- **The health and fitness information you choose to sync**: your details (such as age, height and weight), weight log, food diary, saved foods, workouts and settings. We store these only from devices where you turn on sync, after you see a preview. Legal basis: your **explicit consent**, which you give when you create the account. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.
- **A login cookie** that keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. It is strictly necessary and is not used for tracking.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we do not sell it.

## Your rights

- **Download** everything in your account: Settings → Account → Download account data.
- **Delete** your account and all its data: Settings → Account → Delete account. This takes effect immediately; backups expire within 30 days.
- **Correct** your data by editing it in the app.
- Ask about anything else at [privacy email]. We reply within one month.
- You can complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

## Security

- Passwords are hashed with Argon2id.
- Connections use HTTPS.
- Every request is checked against your own account, so nobody else can read your data.
- We log errors without personal details.
